# PromptProfit — Advertiser Internal Beta Onboarding

**Phase:** Internal Beta Monetization Readiness
**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Audience:** Internal/synthetic test advertisers participating in the
monetization pilot only. **Not for real/external advertisers** — public
advertiser onboarding is blocked until public-release gates pass.

---

## What you are testing

You are testing PromptProfit's ad-serving and billing pipeline for the
ChatGPT browser adapter: a campaign you create can be shown as a small
sponsored banner during a ChatGPT "wait state" (while a response is
generating), and your account is charged per impression and per click
according to your campaign's CPM bid.

## What is synthetic / internal beta

- **Your advertiser account is a test account.** No real company billing
  information, no live Stripe customer, no real invoice is created.
  `advertiserProfiles.stripeCustomerId` remains null — there is no live
  payment integration in this codebase today (confirmed by source review,
  see `MONETIZATION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` Q18).
- **Your campaign budget and CPM bid are test values**, agreed with the
  pilot owner beforehand, not real ad-spend commitments.
- **All impressions/clicks are generated by internal testers**, not real
  ChatGPT users at scale.

## How charges are simulated or limited

- Charges are computed by the same production money-math code
  (`@ad-alt/ledger`'s `LedgerCalculator`) that would run in a real
  deployment — the math itself is real and verified
  (`LEDGER_CONFIDENCE_REPORT.md`), but:
  - No real payment is ever collected — there is no code path anywhere in
    this repository that charges a real credit card or bank account.
  - Your campaign's `budgetMicrocents` acts as a hard spend cap; once
    reached, your campaign is automatically marked `"exhausted"` and stops
    billing (`ledger.service.ts`).
- Every charge produces exactly 3 ledger rows (`advertiser_charge`,
  `developer_credit`, `platform_fee`) that you can review via
  `query:local-ledger` if you have local stack access, or via a pilot
  report shared by the pilot owner.

## What metrics are visible

- Total impressions/clicks served against your campaign.
- Total `advertiser_charge` accrued (in microcents and USD).
- Whether your campaign is `active`, `paused`, or `exhausted`.
- Fraud-blocked event counts, if any (impressions/clicks that were
  detected as fraudulent and NOT billed to you).

## What is not yet production

- No real payment processor integration (Stripe or otherwise).
- No self-service advertiser dashboard covered by this pilot — campaign
  setup and review is done with the pilot owner directly.
- No refund/credit-adjustment mechanism exists yet if a billing dispute
  arises — any correction during the pilot is a manual, documented
  ledger note, not an automated refund (refunds are schema-only, not
  implemented — `MONETIZATION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` Q17).
- No SLA on ad-serving volume or timing — this is a functional/billing
  pilot, not a performance pilot.

## Privacy guarantees

- Your creative content (headline, body, display URL, click URL) is the
  only advertiser-supplied content shown to testers.
- PromptProfit never shares ChatGPT prompt or response text, chat history,
  page content, or any tester's personal data with your campaign or with
  you — the billing/event pipeline is structurally incapable of carrying
  that data (verified in `MONETIZATION_PRIVACY_REVIEW.md`; the only
  identifiers in any event are server-generated UUIDs and a pseudonymous
  device ID, never page content).
- All ledger/event data shared with you during the pilot is limited to
  aggregate counts and amounts — never raw event-level tester data.
